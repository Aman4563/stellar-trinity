## Title

Primary: Both-Halves Liveness of Deterministic Grounding Instruments, Three-Valued Execution-Fidelity Forensics, and Conformal Admission of Machine Judges for Evaluation-Task Audit.

Alternative: Coverage-Gated Audit Certification with Live Deterministic Instruments and Conditional Machine-Judge Admission.

## Abstract (proposed)

A computing system controls audit certification of executable evaluation tasks using coupled instrument and evidence prerequisites. Each required deterministic grounding instrument must detect a planted-defect fixture and refrain from detecting the defect on a corresponding clean twin during a common run. A failed half blocks reliance. Native execution-trace readers distinguish observed violation, demonstrated coverage without violation, and insufficient evidence, and never infer cleanliness from an absent marker. A judge-admission gate evaluates a machine judge against blinded reference labels under a predeclared conformal procedure and abstains or blocks without required coverage. Further controls bind effective harness configuration, preserve a fixed measured population, preregister reward statistics, isolate rubric drafting before trajectory exposure, and refuse forbidden design-source citations in audit evidence. Bounded descriptor-relative reads protect evidence access, while deterministic and semantic findings may only lower or cap a disposition.

## Apex pillars in independent claims

1. Common-run liveness is a prerequisite to relying on a required deterministic instrument, not an optional regression score: the planted defect must fire, its clean twin must not, and failure of either half blocks.
2. Execution-fidelity standing distinguishes observed violation, demonstrated coverage without violation, and insufficient evidence: absence is never clean, and every required signal must have coverage before a clean rollout determination.
3. Predeclared conformal evaluation against blinded reference labels controls judge admission: absent established empirical coverage, abstention or blocking prevents an unsupported judgment from entering audit evidence.
4. The conjunction controls audit certification of committed executable-task bytes, making the target a machine evidence path rather than a subjective recommendation.

Deliberately excluded from apex are family enumeration, dual-host regeneration, concrete filesystem primitives, suppressed-measurement policy details, configuration adequacy grants, contamination atoms, reward-statistic definitions, probe classes, repetition thresholds, individual diagnostics, rubric temporal isolation, and citation scanning. These remain dependent limitations or specification details. Complete external execution and independent judge replay are not claimed as present services.

Named surfaces must be described only generically in claims: in one implementation the vocabulary includes G-FID, G-CON, G-ADV, G-OPT, G-BUD, CRUCIBLE, FORGE, `.audit/fidelity.yaml`, `.audit/evidence.yaml`, `.memory/hardness.yaml`, `HARDNESS.md`, `trinity.harness-config/v1`, `trinity.trial-conformance/v1`, `JUDGE_`, `HARNESS_CONFIG_`, `PILOT_`, and `FIDELITY_SELF_REPORTED_ONLY`. The eight-family list, eight probes, six statistics, eleven-trial floor, file names, predicate strings, and numeric thresholds are illustrative. Claims enumerate functional relations where needed but do not require those labels or fixed numeral values.

## Design-around foreclosures

1. Keep both twin decisions in the common run; an old fixture pass under different instrument bytes cannot qualify a new audit.
2. Define inert as failure to discriminate, covering both always-clean and always-failing instruments rather than confusing blanket rejection with fail-closed behavior.
3. Require complete signal coverage for a clean rollout; duplicated coverage of one signal cannot fill another signal's absence.
4. Preserve conversion-only and self-reported-only ceilings, even when the submitted record calls itself clean.
5. Retain directory descriptors through traversal; a string containment check alone does not meet the no-follow fallback.
6. Bind the audit root to a trusted project root; a nested candidate-controlled directory with the same name is not an evidence authority.
7. Keep configuration identity, approval, and adequacy separate, and keep trial conformance independent of solver success.
8. Preserve the fixed population and worst-case treatment; exclusion followed by imputation cannot silently improve a release bound.
9. Require statistic bounds before observation and reject a changed set, not merely a changed digest after publication.
10. Require actual grading-path probe execution and block an unrunnable required probe rather than counting it as an absent result.
11. Preserve singleton admission and repeated randomized presentation checks while making transitivity an expressly prospective fallback.
12. Commit the rubric before opening the trajectory channel and refuse excluded drafting provenance, even if later rubric bytes regenerate perfectly.
13. Scan the auditor's own evidence for forbidden design citations, but do not overstate that check as proof of complete runtime secrecy.
