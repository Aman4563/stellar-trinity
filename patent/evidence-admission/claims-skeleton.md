## Title

Primary: Tiered Evidence Admission with Exhaustion Receipts, Supersession Edges, and Blinded Hardness Memory for Benchmark Authoring.

Alternative: Receipt-Gated Evidence Discovery and Blinded Supersession Control for Benchmark Authoring.

## Abstract (proposed)

Systems and methods control evidence supplied to benchmark authoring processes. An evidence domain records immutable closed-schema discovery receipts binding issued queries and returned-set digests. A lower source tier opens only after every substrate of the preceding tier records exhaustion; unresolved access remains a named gap. Discovery does not confer admission authority. Admitted artifacts can retain source bytes and derived renderings under content identities. A catalog retains superseded rows and chainable edges to superseding evidence. An access-controlled authoring domain receives catalog information only through a deterministic projection excluding the supersession edges and their target evidence. Dependent controls include source appraisal, bounded confidence, versioned catalog rows, separate design headroom and measured-success constraints, human-controlled calibration admission, and freshness-bounded corroborated reconnaissance restricted to narrowing output. The architecture separates discovery, evidence integrity, design direction, and execution certification.

## Apex pillars in independent claims

1. Claims 1, 26, and 27 require every-substrate exhaustion before a lower tier opens; a partial success count cannot replace that prerequisite.

2. Each independent claim requires immutable, closed-schema receipts binding the issued query and result-set digest, with an unexhausted lane retained as a gap instead of clean emptiness.

3. Each independent claim denies admission by descent alone, separating search control from evidentiary authority.

4. Each independent claim retains the superseded row and its chainable edge, while an access-controlled authoring domain receives only a deterministic projection excluding both the edge and its target evidence.

Deliberately excluded from apex are specific appraisal tokens, provenance and liveness, source-class resolution rungs, converter and hash choices, GRADE levels and domains, supersession authority classes, the supersession-kind axis, row versions, numeric thresholds, calibration staging, and reconnaissance age or corroboration counts. These remain dependent or specification support rather than replacements for the combined opening, admission, and information-flow controls.

Named surfaces must be described only generically in claims. In one implementation the illustrative vocabulary includes ENGRAM and FORGE, `FORGE_VIEW`, T0 through T7, `engram.receipt/v1`, `engram.identity/v1`, `engram.supersession/v1`, `engram.hardness/v2`, `engram.harvest/v1`, `engram.brief/v2`, `engram.next/v2`, `revoked-by`, `superseded_kind`, `row_version`, `mutable-data-alias`, and the CORPUS_ refusals. Generic functional domain descriptions are distinct from branded instrument roles.

## §101 Alice positioning

Step 2A Prong Two anchors for claims 1, 26, and 27 are the prevention verbs in application section 8.14: prevents lower-tier opening without complete receipts, denies admission by descent, retains invalidation history, and withholds edge and target fields from an access-controlled process. Tie each operation to the resource that cannot open, anchor that cannot enter, or export bytes that cannot carry the forbidden relation.

Claims 8-14 add recomputes and compares bytes, refusing incomplete pairs or mismatched bindings rather than displaying an integrity score. Claims 15-18 cap confidence, prevent unsupported supersession, and exclude the supersession-kind field. Claims 19-22 prevent drift and substitution between distinct constraint quantities. Claims 23-25 deny machine admission and upward authority to advisory outputs.

Step 2B rests on the ordered combination: the receipt cannot admit, the source can invalidate only under specified authority, and the invalidation relation remains retained but inaccessible to the author. A processor, hash, search checklist, or confidence score in isolation is not the asserted inventive concept. A human review policy merely automated on a generic computer is the principal eligibility risk.

The intended effect is deterministic evidence integrity and information-flow control between agent roles. Do not describe the invention as discovering better research or choosing better questions. Contract-only functionality may support a proposed embodiment, but cannot be described as a demonstrated runtime improvement.

## Design-around foreclosures

1. Renaming tiers or changing their count does not remove ordered source-class descent. Keep the every-substrate prerequisite, not an illustrative vocabulary size.

2. Replacing an issued query with topics or a paraphrase does not satisfy verbatim receipt binding. A changed result population requires its own digest-bound receipt.

3. Treating a failed endpoint as an empty success does not satisfy exhaustion. A later successful invocation adds a receipt rather than rewriting the failed one.

4. Discovering incidental low-authority material does not lawfully open a blocked tier or grant anchor authority. Resolution and admission remain separate from search traversal.

5. Hiding only the supersession pointer does not satisfy exclusion if the target evidence remains available in the author projection. A display mask likewise does not establish structural absence.

6. Deleting obsolete rows or their edges does not satisfy retained chainable supersession. A retirement kind is not an extra row state that bypasses the required edge.

7. A pinned program loading moving data still earns the mutable-selector finding. Similar names and identifier-only search hits remain adjacent rather than resolved.

8. A changed hash algorithm can preserve the content-identity structure, but rendering-only storage cannot satisfy paired retention. Nonempty tool declarations do not prove derivation.

9. Increasing composition cannot cure an inadequate evidence target, and neither design quantity can replace execution evidence. A stricter projection cannot authorize a looser success ceiling.

10. A machine label or screening pass cannot insert a staged bundle into the calibration library. Human-controlled copying, verdict assignment, table update, and renewed scope approval remain distinct prerequisites.

11. More mirrored sources do not satisfy independent corroboration. Freshness is assessed against the source timestamp at retrieval, not against the time a summary is viewed.

12. A reconnaissance alert cannot restore a lever or select the authoritative cohort. An independent supersession or registry path must supply its own authority.

## §112 / drafting cautions

Claims 1, 26, and 27 identify processor operations and data structures rather than relying on a bare "controller configured to" formula. Such controller language can still trigger functional-claim scrutiny under Williamson. If introduced, specify the scheduler's every-substrate comparison, sidecar digest recomputation, edge resolution, and projector's exclusion algorithm rather than merely reciting a desired result.

Preserve antecedent basis for the substrate invocation, immediately preceding tier, receipt schema, catalog row, target evidence, and deterministic projection.
