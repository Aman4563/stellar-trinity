# Trinity patent portfolio family map

Draft reconciliation dated 2026-09-21. This map records proposed family boundaries, current draft titles and claim counts, evidence limitations, and human verification gates. It establishes no priority entitlement, patentability conclusion, or completed procedural act.

## Purpose and layout

This repository is the Trinity contract repository on the legacy flat layout with the single implicit work `main`. The parent application lives at `patent/application.md` with `patent/claims-skeleton.md`. Each `patent/<slug>/` subdirectory named below is a member of that one work's patent family, a planned divisional or continuation subject to human support and prosecution review, not a separate registered work. No row is added to any works registry. This file names every member and resolves the ENGRAM Phase S surface-subdirectory registry rule for this legacy layout without inventing a registry or additional works. Scribe is capped at draft because this repository carries no `.memory/` ledger.

Each sibling contains an `application.md` and a `claims-skeleton.md`. The seven family directories are `tamper-evident-governance`, `release-authority`, `council-gate`, `identity-lanes-epochs`, `evidence-admission`, `auditor-instruments`, and `author-hardening`. The parent section 2 remains "Not Applicable"; sibling section 2 passages describe a prospective relationship, not a benefit claim. Statutory title-block legends identify intended forms only. A future filing route would require human review of original support, new matter, inventorship, continuity, restriction, and consonance.

## Jurisdiction notes

US: MPEP 802 and 806 distinctness and 37 CFR 1.141 to 1.146 would guide restriction analysis, separate from the conditions for 35 U.S.C. § 121 protection. MPEP 2106 and the 2024 AI subject-matter eligibility update, Examples 47 to 49, would guide the specific technical integration argument. Recentive Analytics v. Fox (Fed. Cir. 2025) presents risk for classifier-shaped claims that merely apply generic machine learning; the drafts should retain concrete byte bindings, authority boundaries, and denied machine transitions. MPEP 608.01(p) and 2164.02 require truthful treatment of prophetic examples in present or conditional tense. Williamson v. Citrix raises 35 U.S.C. § 112(f) exposure for an unexplained "controller configured to" result, so corresponding algorithms, data structures, and ordered checks require explicit support.

EP: Article 82, Rule 44, and Guidelines F-V would govern unity through special technical features. Guidelines G-II 3.3 and 3.3.1 would govern mathematical methods, computer-implemented inventions, and AI features through their specific technical purpose and effect. The portfolio strategy would arrange separate EP applications for distinct groups at the national-phase stage rather than await a posteriori unity objections, subject to counsel's choice of the proper procedural route; a folder boundary alone does not establish unity or entitlement to a separate application.

IN: Section 3(k), Ferid Allani v. Union of India (Delhi High Court, 2019), and Microsoft Technology Licensing v. Assistant Controller, 2023:DHC:3342, would frame technical-effect analysis. Each independent claim requires an explicit paragraph identifying its technical problem, technical solution, and technical benefit without asserting a measured result. Novel hardware is not required merely to establish that technical effect; generic automation or an abstract administrative policy remains insufficient as the asserted contribution.
